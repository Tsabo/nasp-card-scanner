Getting a clean read
  • Lay the card flat in even light and avoid glare across the bubbles.
  • Fit the whole card in the frame, including the black marks down the left edge. Those marks are how the reader finds each row.
  • Blue, red and green cards all work, at any angle or rotation, even upside down. Straight on reads best.
  • A clip over the top of the card is fine as long as the left-edge marks for every row are visible.
  • Dark pencil fills read most reliably. Erase changed answers completely, or the card gets flagged.
