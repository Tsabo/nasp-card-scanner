# NASP Card Scanner

Reads NASP practice score cards from a photo and keeps a log of them for export to a spreadsheet.

## Language

**Arrow**:
One shot, scored 0 to 10, or left blank.

**Round**:
Five arrows shot together. A scorecard has six: Rounds 1 to 3 from 10 meters and Rounds 4 to 6 from 15 meters.
_Avoid_: End (some printed cards still say "End")

**Scorecard**:
One archer's six rounds (30 arrows) on one date, out of 300.
_Avoid_: Round, card, practice round

**Practice log**:
The scorecards saved in this browser.

**Shooter #**:
The five-digit number that identifies an archer on the card.
_Avoid_: Shooter number, archer ID
